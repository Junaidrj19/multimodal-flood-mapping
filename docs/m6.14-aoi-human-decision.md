## 1. Executive Recommendation

**Recommended: Option A — Adopt the 105.858 km corridor with partial-coverage disclosure.**

The corridor defined by index 49, reach `40801674`, with a 1,000 m buffer, 105.858 km centreline length, and 206.1509 km² at buffer, is scientifically defensible as the hydrological terminus of the upper Trishuli (Strahler order-5). It satisfies the M6.9 §4.2 inset clearance (6,763.1 m from swath edge), enables the full `{500, 1000, 2000}` m buffer sensitivity set, and requires the minimum hydrological judgement — it is the last reach of the homogeneous order-5 stem. The 36.6 % unobserved portion must be disclosed transparently on all surfaces.

## 3. Evidence and Limitations

- **Index 49, reach `40801674`**: Main-stem length 105.858 km; area at 1,000 m buffer: 206.1509 km²
- **Delivered imagery coverage**: 63.4 % (both Sentinel-1 RTC products, measured per-pixel valid fraction = 1.000000)
- **Unobserved portion**: 36.6 % (no delivered pixels inside the AOI)
- **Clearance from Sentinel-1 scene footprint**: 6,763.1 m (inset satisfied with 27× margin)
- **Buffer sensitivity**: `{500, 1000, 2000}` m all produce valid single Polygons inside scene footprint; 250 m inset satisfied at each width
- **Strahler order**: Index 49 is the last reach of the homogeneous order-5 stem (49 of 49 reaches at `ORD_STRA` 5)
- **Downstream boundary**: Terminates where the order-5 stem ends and a co-equal 5,009.5 km² tributary (75 % of stem) joins at reach `40801673`

### 3.2 Interpretations and Assumptions
- The 1,000 m buffer is carried over from M6.2–M6.5 for comparability; it is not operator-ratified (M6.9 §5.1 item 2; M6.10 §5.1 item 2)
- The Strahler-order transition at index 49 is a hydrographic proxy for "where the upper river ends"; HydroRIVERS carries no river-name attribute
- 36.6 % unobserved AOI is empirically measured from the two delivered RTC GeoTIFFs; valid-pixel fraction = 1.000000 where pixels exist
- The 250 m inset is defined relative to the same-track pair's common swath footprint (M6.9 §4.2); clearance = 6,763.1 m ≫ 250 m

## 5. Partial-Coverage Decision

The chosen corridor has **63.4 % delivered imagery coverage** and **36.6 % unobserved area**. The valid-pixel fraction over observed pixels is 1.000000 across all band/raster combinations (VV, VH pre and post).

**Decision**: Accept partial coverage with mandatory disclosure. The AOI polygon must not imply full observational coverage. Every surface — dashboard, reports, provenance manifests — must explicitly state:

> "This AOI has 63.4 % delivered RTC coverage with 1.000000 valid-pixel fraction where covered. 36.6 % of the AOI has no delivered pixels."

**Conditions for adoption**:
1. Human ratification of extent policy (D1): adopt index 49 corridor
2. Human ratification of buffer (D2): confirm 1,000 m
3. Human ratification of inset policy (D3): confirm 250 m inset against swath footprint
4. Human authorization of disclosure: mandatory partial-coverage statement on all surfaces
5. Optional: Human authorization of additional acquisition to reach 100 % coverage
