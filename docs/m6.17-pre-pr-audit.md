## 5. Four notions kept distinct

### 5.1 Where they appear

* **Artifacts:** `rtc_change_summary.json` carries a window-scope block
  (`total_pixels`, `valid_fraction`, `candidate_fraction_of_grid`,
  `candidate_grid_area_km2`, `scope_note`) and an `aoi_scope` block with
  `denominator_definitions`; `candidate_grid_area_km2 = 4.5269` is the only
  window-scope area and is not the extent.
* **Dashboard:** `dashboard/app.py::_render_aoi_scope` renders five labelled
  rows — Study extent (AOI polygon) 206.1507 / Delivered (AOI ∩ raster window)
  130.6416 / Unobserved 75.5090 / Validly observed 130.6418 / Darkening
  candidates 4.5269 — each with a share column and an explicit status
  (`Study extent — context, not evidence`, `Observed / derived`,
  `Unknown — not unaffected, flood-free or undamaged`,
  `Change candidate — NOT confirmed flood or damage`). The approved disclosure
  renders as a `st.warning` above the table, the primary rate is stated against
  the valid in-extent pixel count, the share-of-extent column is captioned a
  lower bound, and the recorded denominator definitions are in an expander. The
  window warning now states how many window pixels fall outside the extent.
* **Validator:** `discover_m63_artifacts()` re-read from the workspace returns
  `state=valid`, `usable=true`, **36 checks / 0 failed / 0 problems**,
  configured-AOI problems `[]`, delivered fraction `0.6337192324738476`,
  unobserved `0.36628076752615235`; it recomputes AOI-scope counts from the
  masks, cross-checks the summary, enforces candidate ⊆ AOI, valid ⊆ AOI and
  candidate ⊆ valid, and refuses any result whose delivered/valid area exceeds
  the full extent.
* **Documentation:** `docs/data-contract.md §0.1.1`, `docs/m6.15 §3`,
  `docs/m6.16 §2, §3.3, §9` and the M6.3 provenance notes keep extent /
  delivered / valid / candidate distinct with named denominators. No document
  reviewed describes the extent as observed, or a window denominator as an AOI
  rate.

### 5.2 Residual risk (low, presentational)

Two adjacent dashboard rows mix measurement types: `Delivered` is a geometric
polygon intersection, `Validly observed` is a pixel-centre estimate, and the
estimate is marginally larger than the geometric intersection. A reviewer who
divides the two will "discover" that valid > delivered. **Smallest safe fix:**
add “pixel-centre estimate” to the `Validly observed` row label (or a one-line
caption under the table) and assert the label in `tests/test_dashboard_app.py`.
No number changes.
