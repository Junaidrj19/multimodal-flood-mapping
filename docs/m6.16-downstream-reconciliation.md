## 3. Phase 3 — M6.3 regenerated

### 3.1 What changed in the stage, and why

The stage is now `m6.3-rtc-change-candidates-0.2.0`. The version was bumped
because the artifact contract genuinely changed; leaving `0.1.0` on a
differently-scoped product would be the same class of mislabelling this
milestone exists to remove.

1. **The free-text `--aoi-id` mislabelling hazard is closed.** The option is
   gone. `load_configured_aoi_binding()` reads `acquisition.aoi` from
   `configs/data.yaml`, **enforces** the configured SHA-256 against the geometry
   bytes, and requires the provenance sidecar to agree on both identifier and
   digest. The geometry used for masking and the digest written into the outputs
   come from the same verified bytes, so a run cannot stamp an extent it did not
   use. An optional `--expect-aoi-id` lets a caller *assert* which extent it
   intended; it can never set one.
2. **Products are AOI-scoped.** The AOI is rasterized onto the delivered grid by
   pixel centre (`all_touched=false`, no resampling, no geometry repair) and
   intersected into the validity mask *before* the candidate rule runs. A
   candidate is therefore an observed, in-extent pixel by construction.
3. **`rtc_aoi_mask.tif` is new and required.** The candidate mask has a single
   not-observed class (255), which cannot distinguish "outside the study extent"
   from "inside the extent but not observed". The AOI mask keeps that
   distinction recoverable.
4. **Two scopes, never one number.** The summary reports delivered-raster-window
   array tallies and an `aoi_scope` block separately, each with named
   denominators, plus a `denominator_definitions` map written into the artifact.
5. **Refusals added:** an AOI that intersects no pixel centre is refused rather
   than emitting an all-nodata product that could read as "no change"; and the
   writer refuses to emit candidates outside either the valid mask or the AOI.

## 5. Phase 5a — M6.6 dashboard reconciled

The validator (`src/floodmap/dashboard/realdata.py`) was moved to the 0.2.0
contract: `rtc_aoi_mask.tif` is a required file, its band name and grid are
checked against the other three rasters, AOI-scoped counts are **recomputed from
the masks** and cross-checked against the summary, and a complete `aoi_scope`
block is mandatory. Two new invariants are enforced from the rasters
themselves — candidates must lie inside the AOI mask, and the validity layer
must be AOI-scoped — alongside the pre-existing candidate ⊆ valid check. A
sanity check refuses any result whose delivered or valid area exceeds the full
extent area.

The dashboard presentation gained an **"Observed support within the study
extent"** section that always shows the three distinct areas together — study
extent, delivered, validly observed — plus candidates, each with its share of
the extent and an explicit status column (`Study extent — context, not
evidence` / `Observed / derived` / `Unknown — not unaffected, flood-free or
undamaged` / `Change candidate — NOT confirmed flood or damage`). The approved
disclosure renders above the table, the primary candidate rate is stated against
the valid in-extent pixel count, the share-of-extent column is labelled a lower
bound, and the recorded denominator definitions are available in an expander.
The previous window-wide warning was corrected: it no longer calls the window
"the assembled AOI bounding grid" but states that it is a bounding-box crop that
is **not** the study extent and reports how many of its pixels fall outside.

## 9. Denominator definitions (as written into the artifacts)

- **`aoi_geometric_area_km2`** — area of the full approved AOI polygon in
  EPSG:32645. This is the study extent, **not** an observed area, and must never
  be described as observed.
- **`aoi_delivered_area_km2`** — geometric intersection of the AOI polygon with
  the delivered raster window bounding box. Delivery is where pixels exist,
  which is distinct from where those pixels are valid.
- **`aoi_unobserved_area_km2`** — AOI area the delivered raster window does not
  reach. No observation exists there; it is unknown, not a negative finding.
- **`aoi_valid_area_km2`** — pixel-centre count × native pixel area, for centres
  inside the AOI with a jointly valid pre and post VV+VH observation. A
  rasterization estimate, not a geometric intersection.
- **`candidate_fraction_of_aoi_valid_pixels`** — primary candidate rate.
  Denominator is the observed, valid, in-extent pixel count: the only support
  the rule could actually evaluate.
- **`candidate_fraction_of_aoi_geometric_area`** — candidate area over the full
  AOI area. A lower bound only: 36.6% of the AOI is unobserved, so this is not a
  prevalence rate over observed land.
- **`window_pixels_outside_aoi`** — delivered window pixels whose centres fall
  outside the AOI. Excluded from every AOI-scoped figure and from the candidate
  mask.
- **Window-scope top-level fields** (`total_pixels`, `valid_fraction`,
  `candidate_fraction_of_grid`, `candidate_grid_area_km2`) — mechanical array
  tallies over the delivered raster window, which is a bounding-box crop and is
  **not** the AOI.

Joint validity, applied per source and per band: GDAL mask valid **and** not the
declared nodata (`-32768`) **and** finite **and** strictly positive linear SAR
power. Zero, negative, NaN and infinity are not dark-water evidence. Rasterization
uses pixel centres with `all_touched=false`; no resampling or geometry repair
occurs anywhere in the chain.
